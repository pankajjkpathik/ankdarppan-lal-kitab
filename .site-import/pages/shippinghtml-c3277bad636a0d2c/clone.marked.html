Because the Report is delivered online, we can deliver to customers anywhere in India and abroad, as long as you can receive WhatsApp messages or email. Payments are accepted in Indian Rupees through Razorpay.

Delivery is complete once the Report has been sent to the WhatsApp number or email address you provided. Please save the PDF on your phone or computer, as we do not keep a copy of your Report after delivery (see our Privacy Policy).

If you entered a wrong number or email, contact us from your correct number or email with your payment ID, and we will send the Report there. We are not responsible for a Report delivered to a wrong number or email that you entered.

9.Delayed delivery

If your Report has not reached you within 48 hours, contact us and we will send it immediately. Orders are not cancellable or refundable (see our Refund & Cancellation Policy). If we are ever unable to prepare or deliver your Report at all, the amount you paid will be returned.
