This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to the Sampurna Lal Kitab Report (₹581, inclusive of GST) purchased from Ank Darppan through www.ankdarppan.com. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions. Please read it before you pay.

1.No cancellation, no refund

Each Report is calculated and prepared individually from the birth details you submit, and work on it begins as soon as your payment is received. Because it is a personalised digital product that cannot be returned or resold, all sales are final:

  • Orders cannot be cancelled after payment.
  • No refund is given for a change of mind, or because you disagree with or do not like the predictions, scores or remedies in the Report.
  • No refund is given if events, results or timings do not happen as you expected. Astrology offers guidance, not guarantees (see section 8 of our Terms).
  • No refund is given where the Report was prepared using incorrect birth details that you supplied, or delivered to a wrong WhatsApp number or email that you entered.

2.Corrections

  • Mistake made by us (for example, we entered your birth details incorrectly, or a page is missing or the file will not open): we will correct the Report and re-send it free of charge within 24–48 hours.
  • Mistake in the details you gave us (wrong date, time or place of birth): tell us as soon as possible. If we have not yet prepared the Report, we will use the corrected details at no cost. If the Report has already been prepared, a corrected Report can be prepared for an additional fee, which we will tell you before we start.
  • Wrong WhatsApp number or email: contact us from your correct number or email with your Razorpay payment ID within 24–48 hours of delivery and we will send the Report there.

3.Payment errors

This policy does not prevent the return of money that was taken by mistake. If you were charged twice for the same order, or money was deducted but the payment failed and no order was created, the extra or failed amount will be returned to your original payment method. Failed payments are usually reversed automatically by Razorpay or your bank within 5–7 working days. If we are unable to prepare or deliver your Report at all, the amount you paid will be returned in the same way.

4.Contact

For a correction or a payment error, contact us on WhatsApp or email with your name and Razorpay payment ID. We respond within 24–48 hours.
