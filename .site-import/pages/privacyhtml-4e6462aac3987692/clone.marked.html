This Privacy Policy explains how Ank Darppan collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit www.ankdarppan.com or order a Sampurna Lal Kitab Report. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

1.Who we are

Ank Darppan, a proprietorship of Anamika Pathik, HNO 48, Near Gurudwara, Sunny Side, Solan, Himachal Pradesh 173212, India, decides how your personal data is used (the "Data Fiduciary" under Indian law). You can reach us at ankdarppan@gmail.com or 93173 65025.

2.What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Full name and genderTo prepare and personalise your Report
Date, exact time and place of birthTo calculate your kundli and Lal Kitab charts. Place of birth is converted to latitude and longitude
WhatsApp number and email addressTo deliver the Report, confirm details and answer your questions
Payment information: amount, Razorpay payment ID, payment status, and the name, email and phone entered at checkoutTo confirm your order and keep tax records

We do not collect your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net banking password. These are entered directly on Razorpay's secure page. If you order a Report for someone else, please make sure you have their permission to share their details with us.

3.How we use your data

  • To prepare your personalised Report and deliver it on WhatsApp and email.
  • To contact you if a birth detail is missing or unclear.
  • To process payments and corrections, and to maintain accounting and tax records required by law.
  • To respond to your questions, complaints and requests.
  • To send you information about other reports or offers only if you have opted in. You can opt out at any time by replying "STOP" or writing to us.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

4.Consent

By submitting your details and completing payment, you give us consent to use your data for the purposes in section 3. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. If you withdraw consent before your Report is delivered, we will not be able to prepare it, and our Refund & Cancellation Policy will apply.

5.Who we share your data with

We share only what is needed to provide the service:

  • Razorpay, to process payments.
  • WhatsApp and email, to deliver your Report and talk to you.
  • Government or law enforcement authorities, only when required by law.

6.Sample pages on our Website

We may show pages from real Reports as samples, so that buyers can see what they will receive. We do this only with the client's permission, and we always hide the client's name, date, time and place of birth, and any other details that could identify them. We will never show your Report as a sample without your permission.

7.How long we keep your data

  • Birth details and your Report: we do not save them in any database or file. They are used only to prepare your Report, and the working copy is deleted once the Report has been delivered. Please save the PDF on your device, because we cannot re-send it from our records.
  • WhatsApp and email conversations: the details you send us and the Report we deliver stay in our chat or email thread with you, as they do in yours. You can ask us to delete our copy of these messages at any time.
  • Payment records: payment details are held by Razorpay under its own policy. We keep only the invoice and payment reference that tax and accounting laws require us to keep.

8.Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including password-protected accounts, limiting access to people who need it to prepare your Report, and using secure (HTTPS) connections. Payments are processed by Razorpay, which is PCI-DSS compliant. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

9.Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • get a summary of any personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data, including our copy of your WhatsApp or email messages;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us at ankdarppan@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at 93173 65025. We will respond within 24–48 hours.

10.Children

Our service is meant for people aged 18 and above. Reports for a child under 18 may be ordered only by a parent or legal guardian, who gives consent on the child's behalf. We do not knowingly collect a child's data without this consent, and we do not use children's data for marketing or tracking.

11.Cookies and third-party content

This Website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address when the page loads. Links to Razorpay, WhatsApp or other websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its "Last updated" date. If we make a significant change in how we use your data, we will inform you before it applies to you.
