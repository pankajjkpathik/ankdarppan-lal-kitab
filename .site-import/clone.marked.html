Lal Kitab houses depend on birth time, so an accurate time gives the best report. If you only know an approximate time, mention it when you order and we will contact you before preparing the report.

How will I receive the report?

As a PDF sent to your WhatsApp and email. You can read it on your phone or print it.

What language is the report in?

English, with Hindi names for every chart, planet, house and concept, so the Lal Kitab terms stay familiar.

Are the remedies expensive?

No. Lal Kitab upay are simple acts like feeding animals, donating specific items on specific days, or keeping an object with you. The report also explains the rules, such as doing one remedy at a time for 40 to 43 days.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Each report is prepared personally from your birth details, so orders cannot be cancelled or refunded after payment. Please check your details before paying. If we make a mistake in your report, we correct it and re-send it free within 24–48 hours. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Is my birth data kept private?

Yes. Your details are used only to prepare your report and are not stored after it is delivered, so please save your PDF. Any sample pages we show have the name and birth details hidden.
